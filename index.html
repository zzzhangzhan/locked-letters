Letterlocking

Send a letter that locks itself

Letterlocking is the craft of folding and securing a sheet of paper so it becomes its own envelope. Since the 13th century, folds, tucks and slits have kept letters from being read without leaving a trace.

Write a virtual letter, lock it with a historic fold, and send it as a link. Your reader has to unfold it to read it.

Unlocking History Research Group

Each lock here follows one documented by the group, who have catalogued over 7,000 letters from more than 70 archives and give every technique a UH number.
